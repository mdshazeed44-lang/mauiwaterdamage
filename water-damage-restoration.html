What is water damage restoration?

Rapid response that saves your home — and your budget

Water damage restoration is the professional process of removing water, drying the structure, preventing mold, and repairing the damage after a leak or flood. The faster it starts, the more of your property can be saved.

At Maui Water Damage Pros, we combine island experience with certified methods and commercial-grade equipment. Every job follows the same disciplined process — inspect, extract, dry, verify, restore — and every step is documented for your insurance claim.

Common causes we handle

Where Maui water damage usually starts

Burst & leaking pipes

Aging plumbing, high pressure and corrosion cause sudden leaks inside walls and under floors.

Appliance overflows

Washing machines, dishwashers and water heaters fail and flood rooms quickly.

Roof & storm leaks

Heavy rain and trade-wind storms drive water through roofs, windows and lanais.

Flooding & flash floods

Ground water and flash flooding overwhelm ground floors and crawl spaces.

Sewage backups

Blocked drains push contaminated water back into homes — a serious health hazard.

Hidden slow leaks

Small, unnoticed leaks cause rot and mold over time behind walls and cabinets.

Our 5-step restoration process

Exactly what happens when you call

Emergency contact & dispatch

You reach a real person 24/7. We gather key details and dispatch the nearest certified crew immediately.

We locate the source, measure moisture with thermal cameras and meters, and classify the water category.

Water extraction

Cleaning & restoration

Why every hour counts

The water-damage timeline

Water damage gets worse — and more expensive — the longer it waits. Here's what happens if it isn't treated.

Within minutes

Water spreads across floors and is absorbed into walls, furniture and belongings.

Hours 1–24

Drywall swells, furniture warps, and metal surfaces begin to tarnish and corrode.

Day 1–2

Mold and mildew begin to grow; a musty odor sets in across the affected area.

Week 1+

Structural damage and widespread mold make restoration far costlier and slower.

Good to know

Water damage restoration FAQs

Watch for damp or discolored patches on walls and ceilings, peeling paint, a musty smell, warped or buckling flooring, and an unexplained rise in your water bill. Catching these early prevents hidden moisture from turning into mold.

Extraction takes a few hours; structural drying usually runs 3–5 days depending on the materials. Full restoration with repairs can take one to three weeks. You'll get a clear timeline after our on-site assessment.
