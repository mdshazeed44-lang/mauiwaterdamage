Water damage questions, answered

We answer calls 24/7 and arrive on-site within 60 minutes in most areas of Maui, including Kahului, Wailuku, Kihei and Lahaina. Fast response limits structural damage and prevents mold, which can start growing within 24–48 hours.

Yes. We document all damage with photos and moisture readings, communicate directly with your adjuster, and bill your carrier whenever possible — so most homeowners pay only their deductible. We handle the paperwork from first notice of loss to final restoration.

Most residential projects in Maui range from $1,200 to $5,500 depending on the size of the affected area, how long the water sat, and whether mold is present. We always provide a free, no-obligation on-site assessment before any work begins.

Sudden, accidental water damage — like a burst pipe, appliance overflow, or storm-driven roof leak — is typically covered by standard homeowners policies. Gradual leaks and ground flooding usually need separate coverage. We'll help you understand and document your specific coverage.

If it's safe: shut off the water source, turn off electricity to affected areas, and move valuables to a dry space. Don't use a household vacuum on standing water. We'll guide you step by step on the phone until we arrive.
