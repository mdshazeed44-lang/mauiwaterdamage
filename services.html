What we do

One certified team for every kind of water damage

From emergency extraction to full reconstruction, we restore Maui homes and businesses with a single, accountable crew — and we handle your insurance claim along the way.

Call 24/7 Emergency Line Get a Free Assessment

01 — Emergency

Water Extraction & Removal

When water is pooling, every minute matters. Our truck-mounted and portable extractors remove hundreds of gallons quickly to stop damage from spreading into subfloors and walls.

  • 24/7 emergency dispatch across Maui
  • Source identification & water shut-off
  • Content protection & pack-out if needed

02 — Dry Out

Structural Drying & Dehumidification

Extraction alone isn't enough — moisture hides inside walls and framing. We deploy air movers and commercial dehumidifiers, then monitor moisture daily until readings confirm your structure is truly dry.

  • Moisture mapping with thermal imaging
  • Daily monitoring & documented readings
  • Prevents warping, swelling & hidden mold

03 — Health

Mold Remediation

Maui's humidity means mold can take hold fast. We contain the affected area, filter the air with HEPA scrubbers, remove contaminated materials, and treat surfaces with antimicrobials to keep your family healthy.

  • Full containment & negative air pressure
  • HEPA filtration & antimicrobial treatment
  • Post-remediation verification available

04 — Storm

Flood & Storm Damage Cleanup

Tropical storms, flash floods and heavy rain can overwhelm a home in minutes. We respond rapidly to remove flood water, salvage what we can, and stabilize your property against further damage.

  • Flood water removal & debris cleanup
  • Emergency board-up & tarping
  • Sanitizing of flood-affected areas

05 — Biohazard

Sewage & Biohazard Cleanup

Sewage backups and contaminated "black water" are serious health hazards. Our trained technicians safely remove waste, disinfect every surface, and dispose of hazardous materials to code.

  • Category 3 water & sewage handling
  • Hospital-grade disinfection
  • Safe disposal & odor removal

06 — Rebuild

Reconstruction & Repairs

Once everything is dry and clean, we rebuild. From drywall and flooring to cabinetry and paint, we restore your property to its pre-loss condition — all under one roof.

  • Drywall, flooring & trim replacement
  • Painting & finish carpentry
  • One accountable team, no subcontractor chaos
